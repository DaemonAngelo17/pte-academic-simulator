In today's lecture we'll consider another scientific process: the creation of standardized recipes. If a chef is aiming to produce a collection of recipes for a cookery book, or devising a formula that must be followed precisely by other culinary staff, attention to detail is paramount. Many professional chefs utilize a test kitchen, which functions more like a chemical laboratory than a domestic kitchen. During testing, every ingredient is measured to the gram, and cooking temperatures and times are methodically recorded and repeated to establish consistency. Once perfected, the recipe is tested by a competent home cook to ensure the instructions are clear and reproducible. Many chefs dislike this rigorous procedure, noting that it restricts their natural spontaneity and artistic creativity.
