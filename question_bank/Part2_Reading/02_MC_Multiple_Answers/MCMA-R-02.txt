PASSAGE:
Plant circadian rhythms regulate diverse physiological processes, including photosynthetic gene expression, floral scent emission, and leaf movement. Synchronized by photoreceptors that detect dawn and dusk light wavelengths, these internal biological clocks anticipate diurnal cycles rather than merely reacting to environmental shifts. Disruption of these circadian clocks has been demonstrated to reduce reproductive fitness and compromise drought resilience in agricultural crops.
