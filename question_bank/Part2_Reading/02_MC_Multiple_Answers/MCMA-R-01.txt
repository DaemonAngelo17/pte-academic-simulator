Cities like Harappa and Mohenjo-daro featured grid-pattern street layouts, multi-story brick dwellings, and intricate covered drainage channels running beneath paved streets. Furthermore, standardized weights and measures found throughout the region indicate centralized administrative coordination and active trade networks with Mesopotamia.
