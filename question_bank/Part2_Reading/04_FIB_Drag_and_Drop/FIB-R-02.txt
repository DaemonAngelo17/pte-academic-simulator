Cellular respiration occurs in three distinct stages: glycolysis, the citric acid cycle, and oxidative phosphorylation. Glycolysis takes place in the [cytoplasm] of the cell and produces pyruvate without requiring oxygen. In contrast, subsequent stages occur within the [mitochondria], generating substantial quantities of adenosine triphosphate through aerobic [metabolic] pathways.

DISTRACTORS: chloroplast, ribosome, synthetic
