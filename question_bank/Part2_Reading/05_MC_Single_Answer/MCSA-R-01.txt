PASSAGE:
Glaciologists measuring ice core samples extracted from deep ice sheets in Antarctica have constructed atmospheric carbon dioxide records spanning 800,000 years. Trapped air bubbles in successive snowfall layers preserve ambient atmospheric gas concentrations at the exact epoch the ice compacted. These records unequivocally reveal that contemporary atmospheric CO2 levels exceed any historical threshold documented over the last eight hundred millennia.
