Historically, economic growth was measured strictly by Gross Domestic Product. However, ecological economists emphasize that GDP fails to account for resource depletion, environmental degradation, and unequal income distribution. Modern alternatives like the Genuine Progress Indicator adjust economic figures downward when pollution and resource exhaustion occur, offering a more realistic assessment of societal well-being.
